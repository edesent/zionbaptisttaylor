This is an ordinary paragraph. Write the way you would in any document, and leave a blank line between paragraphs. You can make a word **bold** or *italic*, and you can link to another page on the site, like our [Statement of Faith](/beliefs), or to an outside website.

## This Is a Section Heading

Start a line with two pound signs and a space to make a heading. Headings help readers scan the article, and they help Google understand what the article covers.

> Your word is a lamp to my feet And a light to my path.
> -- Psalm 119:105 (NKJV)

Start a line with a greater-than sign to set off a Scripture quotation. Put the reference on its own line beginning with two hyphens, and it becomes the citation underneath.

### A Smaller Heading

A bulleted list looks like this:

- The first item
- The second item
- The third item

A numbered list looks like this:

1. Read the passage
2. Observe what it says
3. Apply what it means

---

Three hyphens on a line by themselves draw a divider. To publish a real article, set the draft line to false (or remove it), and the article will appear on the live site.
